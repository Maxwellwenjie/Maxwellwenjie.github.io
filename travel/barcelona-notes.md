A short city trip with one rule: out before the city wakes up.

## The Gothic Quarter

Before 9 a.m. it is quiet stone and closed shutters. By 11 it is a different place entirely.

## Eixample

The grid is best understood from above — find a rooftop bar and look at the chamfered
corners. Suddenly the plan makes sense.

## What I would skip

The obvious tourist buses. The metro gets you everywhere faster and you see more.
