Three days, one rental car, and a route that turned out to be more about the driving
than the destinations.

## Marseille

Worth one morning. The Vieux-Port before 8 a.m. is a different city.

## The Luberon

The hill villages are the point. Gordes at midday is a car park; Gordes at 7 a.m. is why
you came.

## Practical notes

- Fuel up before leaving the autoroute.
- Village roads are narrower than the map suggests.
- Most restaurants close between 14:30 and 19:00 — plan around it.
