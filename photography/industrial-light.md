Assembly halls have some of the most interesting light in any building — and almost
nobody photographs them.

## Why it works

Large diffuse sources overhead, deep shadows underneath machinery, and a colour temperature
that shifts between sodium and LED across the hall.

## Access

Sunday shifts. Fewer people, permission is easier, and the light is the same.
