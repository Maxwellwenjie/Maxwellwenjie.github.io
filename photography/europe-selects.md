A running collection, edited down to the frames that still hold up months later.

## Selection criteria

Most travel photos fail because they document rather than see. The ones that survive tend
to have one of three things:

- a strong light direction
- a person doing something unrehearsed
- a frame that is slightly wrong in an interesting way

## Gear

Everything here was shot on a single body and one 35mm equivalent lens. Constraint helps.
