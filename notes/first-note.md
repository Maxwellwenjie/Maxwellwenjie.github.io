I have kept private notes for years. Almost none of them were ever useful again.

## The problem with private notes

They are written for a reader who already has all the context — the meeting, the failure,
the fix. Six months later that reader no longer exists.

## What changes in public

Writing for someone else forces you to state your assumptions. Half of the value is in
discovering that you cannot explain what you thought you understood.

## What this will be

Short, specific, and deliberately unfinished. Not tutorials — working notes.

If something here saves you an afternoon, it was worth writing.
